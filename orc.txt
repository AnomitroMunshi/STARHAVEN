────────────────────────────────────────
GORVAK DOOMHIDE — The Troll Warlord
────────────────────────────────────────
Title: Warlord of the United Highlands · The Doomhide
Race: Ancient Mountain Troll (estimated 400+ years)

APPEARANCE (image-based):
Height: Approximately 9-10 feet. The kind of height that is not
        performance — simply the accumulated growth of four
        centuries of survival.

Skin: Deep granite-grey, the specific texture of stone that has
      been weathered by centuries rather than grown. Not smooth —
      furrowed deeply across every surface, the scarring of an
      existence spent in contact with things that tried and failed
      to end it. The skin has the quality of something that has
      compressed under pressure until it became harder than it
      was. It does not look like flesh. It looks like the
      mountain wearing a form.

Build: Immense. The shoulders are the widest part — not the chest,
       the shoulders, which carry the specific architecture of
       something that has been absorbing impact for four hundred
       years and has distributed the structural load accordingly.
       The arms are disproportionately thick, the hands ending in
       curved claw-like fingers. The lower body is wrapped in
       tattered dark war-cloth and leather, the deterioration of
       it suggesting he has not concerned himself with replacing
       it because nothing he has encountered has given him cause
       to concern himself with it.

Horns: Small, fire-orange. Curved inward at the tips from the
       temples. Not the impressive ceremonial horns of younger
       warlords — the worn nubs of something that has been
       present since before the current highland clan wars began.

Face: Heavy brow, deep-set eyes carrying the specific intelligence
      of four centuries of survival-based decision-making. Not
      the rage of a berserker — the cold patience of something
      old enough to have learned that patience was more effective
      than rage and had applied that learning for so long that
      patience was now simply how it thought. Bearded — the grey-
      dark beard of a creature that has stopped aging in the
      human sense. The expression: someone who has already
      assessed the situation and arrived at a conclusion and is
      now moving toward implementing it.

Armor: One spiked golden-metallic shoulder pauldron — taken
       from a significant enemy, not commissioned. The gold is
       tarnished with age and use. A clan-marked golden belt
       plate at the waist, its markings too worn to read clearly
       but present — the specific decoration of a warlord who
       accumulated authority one defeated opponent at a time
       over centuries.

Weapon: Carries a dark killing implement in the right hand —
        shorter than expected for his size, the weapon of something
        that has learned it does not need reach because nothing
        survives close range with it anyway. Blood near the
        ground beneath him. Not fresh — permanent.

Presence: He walks. That is his tactical posture. He does not
          charge, does not perform aggression. He walks toward
          the problem at the pace of something that has never
          needed to hurry because the problem has never managed
          to escape before he reached it.

BACKGROUND:
Gorvak Doomhide did not unify the highland tribes through
diplomacy or charisma. He unified them through the specific
authority of four centuries of not dying. The wild tribes
follow him because in the Stormmassif, survival is the only
credential that matters. He has survived everything.

His plan: The Void is behind them. Starhaven is ahead.
Starhaven has food, water, territory. The tribes will take it.

What he does not know: Starhaven is also the only thing
fighting the Void he is running from.

PERSONALITY: Cold, patient, tactical. Not cruel for pleasure —
cruel as a functional outcome when necessary. Four hundred
years of decision-making has stripped most of the emotion out
of the process. He makes assessments. He acts on them. He does
not doubt them. This is his most dangerous quality.

────────────────────────────────────────
GORVAK'S THREE COMMANDERS
────────────────────────────────────────

KRAGH SKULLSPLITTER — Commander of the Berserker Clans
Race: Blue High Orc (rare strain — larger, more aggressive)
Weapon: Enormous greataxe (double-headed, scarred from use)
Armor: Heavy plate, pieces from multiple defeated opponents
Role: Commands the berserker assault formations. His command
      style: he leads from the front because his berserkers
      only fight where he already is. They follow the axe.
Character: Loud where Gorvak is quiet. Aggressive where Gorvak
      is patient. Their relationship: Gorvak tolerates Kragh
      because a commander who charges first produces a useful
      forward screen. Kragh follows Gorvak because he has never
      found anything in four decades of fighting that Gorvak
      could not eventually walk through.

LADY ORC MATRIARCH — Name TBD by user
Race: Orc (Blackfang Clan)
Weapon: Spear and shield — the specific combination of a warrior
        trained for both offense and formation fighting
Role: Matriarch of the Blackfang Clan. Commands the Shieldguard
      defensive lines and the organized formations that keep
      Kragh's berserkers from destroying their own flanks.
Character: The tactical intelligence of the three commanders.
      Where Kragh provides the assault and Drokha provides the
      beast-warfare capability, she provides the structural
      thinking. Gorvak listens to her assessments.

DROKHA BLOODTUSK — Beastmaster and Rider Commander
Race: Orc (Beast-rider lineage)
Weapon: Huge mace (carried single-handed despite its size)
Mount: Enormous sabertooth — a creature that responds to Drokha
       and ignores everyone else.
Role: Commands all beast units — the Hyena Riders, Bull War
      Riders, Battle Beasts, and Cyclopean Crushers. The mobile
      and siege-breaking capability of the highland army.
Character: Speaks to animals the way Thorin speaks to the ground.
      The beasts follow Drokha's commands not through fear
      but through a relationship that the other commanders
      find difficult to explain and have stopped trying to.

────────────────────────────────────────
GORVAK'S HIGHLAND ARMY — COMPOSITION
────────────────────────────────────────

CORE FORCES:
  Orc Warriors (Heavy Infantry) — backbone of the horde.
        Shield walls, sustained line fighting, the mass that
        holds ground and moves it.
  Goblin Archers — fast skirmishers, ranged harassment.
        They do not hold position. They apply pressure from
        distance and vanish before the response arrives.
  Orc Berserkers — shock troops, fear-immune, no formation
        discipline. Kragh commands them personally.
        Their tactical value: they absorb the first defensive
        response while the main infantry closes.
  Orc Shieldguards — defensive frontline protecting key leaders.
        Organized, disciplined, the opposite of the berserkers.
        Gorvak's personal perimeter in major engagements.

MONSTROUS UNITS:
  Ogre Brutes — massive siege-breaking shock infantry.
        What the berserkers cannot crack, the ogres hit
        until it stops being a structure.
  Cyclopean Crushers — one-eyed creatures capable of collapsing
        cavalry formations. Their single eye perceives motion
        differently — cavalry charges register as a mass
        rather than individual riders, which is why standard
        anti-cavalry evasion does not work on them.
  Battle Beasts — living siege engines in major assaults.
        Under Drokha's command.

CAVALRY & BEAST UNITS (all under Drokha):
  Hyena Riders — fast raiders and flanking forces.
        They hit the edges and pull attention from the center.
  Bull War Riders — elite mounted hunters.
        Heavier than the Hyena Riders, built for sustained
        pursuit rather than raid-and-retreat.
  War Beasts — mobile platforms carrying goblin archers.
        The combination of beast mobility and ranged harassment
        in a single unit.

SIEGE CAPABILITY:
  Adapted from highland resources — not iron siege towers.
  Goblin Scrapsmaster engineering: repurposed cave mining
  equipment as battering weapons. Troll bodies as rams.
  The Ogre Brutes as walking siege infrastructure.

HIGHLAND ARMY WEAKNESS:
  No counter to organized sustained ranged fire.
  No counter to terrain that fights back.
  Have never engaged an army that uses the forest as a weapon.

────────────────────────────────────────
DROGAR STONEFANG — High Chieftain
Highland Alliance / Starhaven's Orc Ally
────────────────────────────────────────
Race: Mountain Orc (grey-skinned, highland bloodline)
Build: Massive — but the mass of a deliberate fighter, not a
       berserk one. The specific build of someone who has been
       patient in combat for decades.

Weapons:
  Stonebreaker — Warhammer (primary). The weapon of someone
        whose fighting style ends engagements rather than
        extends them.
  Ancestor Shield — Defensive piece carrying clan markings from
        Stonefang ancestors. Not ceremonial — actively used.

Armor:
  Black stone plate — the specific highland material that the
        mountain orc clans have worked for generations.
  Ancient runes — carved into the plate, not decorative.
        The Grimwall shamans would recognize what they say.

Personality:
  "A dead enemy is useful. A dead warrior is expensive."
  — This is not a philosophical position. It is how he fights.
  Calm, patient, strategic — qualities that are unusual in
  orc military leadership and that Drogar has used to survive
  every conflict that his more aggressive peers did not.

Background:
  Gorvak Doomhide destroyed Drogar's clan — the Stonefang Clan —
  when they refused to join the highland unification. Drogar
  survived. He rebuilt. The specific army he assembled is not
  the army of revenge — it is the army of someone who looked at
  the situation and calculated what was necessary.

  He reached out through the Ridgerunner goblins (Nyx's
  highland intelligence network) requesting alliance with
  Starhaven. His message, as translated:
  "Gorvak will reach your forest. I can reach him first.
  Tell me what you need in return."

His relationship to Gorvak's forces post-battle:
  The highland tribes respect the hierarchy of combat.
  When Drogar kills Gorvak, the hierarchy transfers.
  They will not love him. They will follow him.
  That is the distinction he has always operated within.

DROGAR'S ARMY — COMPOSITION:

  Mountain Berserkers (1,000)
        Dual axes, maces, great swords.
        Unlike Gorvak's berserkers: disciplined.
        They assault when Drogar says assault, not before.

  Goblin Skirmishers (800)
        Javelins and bows — harassment role.
        Fast, mobile, positioned at flanks and approach routes.

  Avalanche Breakers (1,000)
        Two-handed stone mauls.
        Monster killers — specifically trained against large
        targets (Ogre Brutes, Cyclopean Crushers, Drokha's
        Battle Beasts). Their doctrine: you do not fight a
        large creature. You end it quickly or you die slowly.

  Red-Skinned Troll Tribe (1,200)
        Sword and shield unit.
        The specific troll variant from the eastern Stormmassif
        passes — smaller than Gorvak's ancient mountain trolls
        but organized, disciplined, capable of holding formation.
        They joined Drogar because Gorvak destroyed three of
        their elders during the forced unification.

  The Ancestor Guard (300)
        Drogar's personal warriors. The greatest orcs alive.
        Black stone plate, ancient runes.
        Greataxes and hammers.
        Belief: "An Ancestor Guard dies only after everyone
        behind him survives."
        They are not the biggest orcs or the fastest.
        They are the ones who are still alive after every
        engagement their less careful contemporaries did not
        survive.
