Warlord Drevak & His Six — The Void's Will Made Flesh
WARLORD DREVAK
Supreme Commander · The Warlord Who Does Not Hurry · Servant of Malgrath · Returned From Death
Black armor not forged but grown — corruption itself having shaped the plating over years into something that fits his body the way a second skeleton fits. A massive glaive resting against one shoulder with the casual ease of a man who has held it so long it became part of how he moves. Void corruption moves beneath the metal plating like liquid darkness searching for its next direction. Tall. Still. With the particular stillness of a man who has stopped needing to prove anything.

Drevak is not a zealot. He is a conclusion. He has reached the arithmetic of the Void — that this world is already dying, that existence itself is the only thing worth fighting for and Malgrath is the only one offering it — and he has found it clarifying rather than frightening. Everything he does flows from the calm of someone who believes he is right in the way mathematics is right.

He was killed at the Havenfire Fortress engagement. He returned. The Void does not lose its instruments permanently. He is larger than he was. The corruption coverage is more complete. The glaive looks heavier. He carries it as if it is lighter than before.
"The King beyond the Void does not fight for land. He fights for existence itself. The only thing that outlasts everything else."

GRATH'UEL THE UNBOUND
Eastern Flank Commander · Former Orc General · Void-Augmented Beyond Return · Twelve Feet
A former orc general whose corruption augmentation has crossed the threshold that most Void-touched soldiers never survive — not because their bodies fail but because their identity fails first. Grath'Uel retained the general. His tactical awareness is intact. His patience is intact. What the Void took was every limit he previously accepted about what he was willing to do to win.

Twelve feet. Void-plate grown directly into his skin over five years of immersion. His weapon is what remained of his original war maul after five years of corruption contact — something neither maul nor anchor, weighing approximately what a small siege engine weighs, swung with the ease of a man who stopped noticing the weight some time ago.

Commands the eastern flank from the front. His soldiers don't move without him — the eastern cluster's Coordinator defers to his direct orders. The eastern flank is the most disciplined sector in the army because Grath'Uel's attention is always there. He was neutralized at Ashfield. He will return. Corruption-augmented beings at his saturation level do not remain neutralized.
"He moved through his formation with the particular patience of someone who had never once needed to rush toward anything."

SERA NULLBORN
Void Mage Corps Commander · Mobile Position · Former Human Archmage · Identity Partially Retained · Current Location Unknown
Corruption has not degraded Sera Nullborn's intelligence. It removed every hesitation around it. She was an archmage of the second tier — capable, disciplined, ambitious. The Void agreed with her self-assessment completely and then removed the self doing the assessing.

What remained is a mage of exceptional capability operating without ethical constraint. Her Void-bolt barrages produce twelve-mage synchronized strike equivalent from a single caster. She moves constantly in irregular patterns that resist predictive targeting. She recalibrated her entire corps around Runebreaker density within four minutes of the first exchange at Ashfield.

She escaped the Ashfield encirclement through dimensional fold before the wings closed. Nyx Ravel has two cells tracking her current position. They have not reported back in six days.

THE PALE GENERAL
Center-Rear · Unidentified Race · Void-Crystal Encased · Network Architect · Contained — Ongoing
Race unidentified. Entirely encased in Void-crystal growth — visible only as a silhouette that does not move like a biological form. Did not move for four hours and forty-nine minutes at Ashfield. When it finally moved, it targeted the Resonance Corps node cluster — not the main line, not any conventional tactical target. The Soul Link itself.

The Pale General is the Nullborn host's strategic intelligence — responsible for the Coordinator network architecture, the wyvern adaptation, the lane-system learning in the first contact hour. It was carrying a document when Zareth Kull's Seal Order contained it. Velkan Drake read the document for six minutes and sealed the chamber from the outside. Rhegar created a new classification that night. It currently contains one entry.

Status: Contained. Zareth and Velkan are working on what the containment means and how long it holds.

KETH'MAUR THE FLAYED
Western Flank Commander · Former Human Gladiator Champion · Void-Membrane Skin · 47 Wins Undefeated Before Corruption
Once the champion of the Sundered Age colosseum circuit — forty-seven sanctioned fights, retired undefeated, volunteered for a border incursion during the Void's early reach because retirement bored him. The corruption found a body that had spent thirty years being optimized for killing and made very few changes.

The changes it did make: his skin shed entirely during deep immersion and was replaced by Void-membrane — absorbing kinetic impact rather than transmitting it. Conventional blades produce surface cuts that close within eight seconds. Crushing weapons work better. He knows this and fights at close range regardless, because close range is where he has always been comfortable.

Tall, lean, scarred in ways that predate his corruption and in ways that postdate it. The pre-corruption scars are straight. The post-corruption scars are not straight in the way that self-healing Void-membrane is not precise about the direction it closes. Commands the western flank cavalry and the Blood Orc vanguard. Treats the engagement timeline as personal.

VASHARA OF THE SECOND DEATH
Mage Liaison Commander · Formerly Elven · Void Resurrection x2 · Died at Vrak Isles — Walked Back Out Six Weeks Later
Vashara died at the Vrak Isles engagement fourteen months ago. Not almost died. Died — confirmed by Starhaven medical assessment, body recovered, death recorded in the official register. She returned six weeks later walking out of a Void-saturation zone sealed as unrecoverable. The Void's resurrection capability is not widely understood. Vashara is the most documented case in Starhaven's intelligence files.

She is the Nullborn host's liaison between the Void mage battalions and the Coordinator network — coordinating the timing of mage strikes with network-directed infantry advances to produce the two-stage assault pattern Cassia Vayne identified at Ashfield. Full corruption at elven retention stage: the elongated form, the grace repurposed. She moves beautifully and is terrible because of it. Carries no weapon. Does not need one.

BORRAK STONECROWN
Titan Handler · Former Stonemark Dwarven Engineer · 60% Corrupted · Void-Bone Interface Hands · Walks Among Them
The only being in the Nullborn host who can walk among the Void Titans without being incidentally destroyed by their movement and maintain the sustained signal contact required to give them direction. The Titans predate the Coordinator architecture and retain enough residual will to resist pure signal direction. Borrak provides the hybrid — an organic handler who interfaces directly with the Titan signal channels through Void-bone growth that has replaced his original hands over 60% corruption progression.

Formerly a master engineer at the Stonemark Holds. His dwarven structural knowledge translated directly to understanding Titan physiology — he knows exactly where each Titan's corruption growth has weakened its original form and where it has reinforced it. He knows which ones survived Ashfield and what was changed in the process. Starhaven does not. This asymmetry is the specific advantage Rhegar cannot close until the next engagement provides data.

THE FLYING FORTRESS OF THE VRAK ISLES
Pre-Sundering Autonomous Construct · Vrak Isles Origin · Not Nullborn · Allegiance: Void Suppression · Status: Active — Returns East After Contact
Stone and metal. Ancient. Vast. Larger than anything the fleet had ever built, and it moved with the calm of something that had been doing this when the Thalassian League's oldest ancestor was not yet born — and expected to continue doing it after the League's last ship had turned to memory.

Not Nullborn. Not corrupted. A pre-Sundering defense construct of the Vrak civilization, operating autonomously on its original directive: locate and suppress Void corruption. It does not target people. It targets the Void-saturated terrain and entities within its sensor range. The distinction has proven catastrophically important when people are positioned near what it is targeting.

Zephyros — a storm dragon of several centuries — stopped when he saw it. Three seconds of absolute stillness from something that has not been surprised by anything in living memory. Not threat assessment. Recognition. The specific quality of an ancient entity encountering something it has not seen in a very long time and deciding, unhurriedly, how to feel about the fact that it still exists.

At the Vrak Isles engagement: primary weapons disabled by Zephyros's electrical cascade through the targeting architecture. Secondary weapons found a frequency his storm was not covering and began firing at Void-saturated island sections — the shots were hitting the wrong things for the wrong reasons and the outcome was going to be bad regardless of intent. Aurelia jumped from Zephyros onto the upper hull at speed and physically severed the power conduit between the secondary battery positions. The fortress stopped firing. It did not retaliate.

Post-engagement behavior: it matched the fleet's course for four hours — close enough to be visible, far enough to be unambiguous. Not intercepting. Not targeting. Not a threat assessment. Something else. Navigator Crest, thirty-eight years at sea, watched it for a long time. "It's remembering us." After four hours it turned and returned east toward the Vrak Isles.

Cain Drakhar spent eighteen months studying Vrak instruments and power schematics recovered from the Isles. The flying fortress uses magnetic propulsion extended to three-dimensional movement — the same foundational principle Cyrus Vexley independently arrived at for the railway, scaled to something large enough to carry a fortress. Velkan spent forty minutes writing in Cain's workshop without stopping when shown the translated schematics. He said nothing except: "You designed the same machinery."
"It's remembering us." — Navigator Crest, thirty-eight years at sea, watching the fortress match the fleet's course through the morning light.

Assessment Note — Velkan Drake & Cain Drakhar

The flying fortress is not our enemy. It is also not our ally. It is a pre-Sundering machine operating on a directive older than every nation on this continent, and that directive happens to align with ours in one specific way: it targets the Void. The problem is that its target identification cannot account for the difference between a Void Titan standing in corrupted ground and a Starhaven soldier standing in the same ground.

What we know: primary array vulnerable to sustained electrical disruption (Zephyros class). Secondary array vulnerable to physical boarding via upper hull — conduit location confirmed, no defensive response to boarding confirmed at Vrak Isles. It did not retaliate when Aurelia physically severed the conduit. It watched us for four hours afterward and then went home. Crest called it "remembering us." I don't have a better word.

What we don't know: whether there are more. Whether they can be communicated with. Whether the Vrak Isles itself is a factor. Whether the Nullborn know they exist and are avoiding whatever territory they patrol, or whether the Nullborn have not encountered them yet.

The last question matters most. If the Nullborn do not know the flying fortresses exist, they are an asymmetric advantage we have not chosen to deploy. If the Nullborn have already adapted to them, we need to know how.

— V.D. / C.D.
