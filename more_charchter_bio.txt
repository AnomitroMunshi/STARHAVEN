RAYDEN CALE — King of Starhaven · Pillar of the Dawn
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 27 (Year 1047) · Human
Build: Athletic, lean-strong — built for sustained combat not raw power.
       Medium height. The frame of someone who has been fighting since
       he was a teenager and has never stopped.

Face: Sharp, defined. The specific look of someone younger than their
      responsibilities — but only until you look at the eyes.
      Strong jaw. A thin scar on the left cheekbone from before Havenfire.
      Not handsome in a courtly sense. Compelling in the way of people
      who have decided something and mean it.

Hair: Dark brown, short-kept — practical, not styled. Slightly longer
      on top, worn back or loose depending on context.

Eyes: Dark brown. The defining feature. The eyes of someone who is
      always calculating — not cold, not warm, present. The look that
      makes people in the same room feel accounted for.

Expression (default): Contained. Economy over display. Not blank —
      watching. The expression just before a decision is made.

Armor/Clothing:
  - Combat: Dark iron plate, not ceremonial — worn, maintained, functional.
            Dawnguard crimson detail on the shoulder (minimal). The king
            who fights from the ground looks like someone who fights from
            the ground.
  - Casual: Dark coat, riding leather, practical — no decoration.
  - No crown. No cape. Nothing that signals rank through performance.

Weapons (visible):
  - Dawnpiercer: Curved saber on left hip + round shield on back.
                 The saber has a slight golden edge treatment.
  - Kingsbane: Greatsword (6 ft) across the back — enormous, presence-filling,
               enchanted steel with faint temporal resonance shimmer
               (very subtle — not glowing, just slightly wrong-colored).
  - Vossfang Bracer: On right forearm — dark iron bracer with grappling
                     hook mechanism. Functional, not decorative.
  - Onslaught: (Year 1046 onward) Dark pre-Sundering metal transforming
               sidearm — compact in revolver form, carried at right hip.

Mount (optional): Warblade — armored warhorse, dark brown/black,
                  heavy barding, battle-trained.

Color Palette: Deep charcoal, dark iron, crimson accent (minimal),
               dark brown leather. No gold beyond the Dawnpiercer edge.

Mood Keywords: Quiet authority · Economy of presence · Decides and acts ·
               Younger than the weight he carries · King who fights from
               the ground

Do NOT include: Crown, throne, decorative armor, court clothing,
               glowing eyes, dramatic poses. He should look like the
               most capable person in a room full of capable people,
               not the most decorated.

KAEL VARRIC — Minister of Defense · Pillar of the Wall
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 33 (year 1047) · StoneBorn Human
Skin: Has a slightly dense, mineral quality — not stone literally, but the texture of someone whose bloodline runs through earth and granite. Slightly grey-toned compared to standard human baseline.
Build: The Stoneborn build is even denser than a heavily trained human — the weight distribution of something partially geological. Kael at 33 looks like he was built rather than born.
Durability markers: Titan's Vow (absorbs fatal damage, leaves permanent scarring) and Monument of Ruin (partial petrification) — his skin may carry old scarring that healed with a slightly stone-like texture rather than normal scar tissue.
Eyes: Stone-grey. Consistent with the Stoneborn quality.

Face: Broad, hard jaw. The face of a man who has been wearing heavy
      helmets most of his life — the specific weathering of someone
      always at the front. Not expressive. Not meant to be.
      Short beard, grey-shot. Deep-set dark eyes.

Hair: Short, grey-brown. Military cut. May be close-shaved.

Expression (default): Assessing. Not unfriendly — simply measuring
      everything against the standard it would need to meet. If it
      passes: nothing changes. If it doesn't: you'll hear about it.

Armor: Bastion Plate — the heaviest version of Iron Legion standards.
       Black, matte, dense. The plate that was built because Kael
       specified what he needed and the Arsenal built it.
       No decoration. No crest. Just iron.
       Gravetide: enormous rectangular tower shield,
       interlocking edge hooks visible. When he carries it,
       it covers him from chin to boot.

Weapons: Ironwall judgement (one-handed war hammer on back)
         Axe at the hip for close combat.

Mount: DreadMaw —  Armored Rhino

Color Palette: Black iron, matte charcoal, dark grey. No accent colors.
               The most monochromatic of the Eleven.

Mood Keywords: Immovable · The standard everything is measured against ·
               The wall that has never been breached · No ceremony

Do NOT include: Decorative elements, warm expression, relaxed posture.
                Kael at rest looks like Kael in combat. The posture
                does not change.

LYRIA VALEN — Pillar of the Eye · Commander of the Silent Wardens
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 127 years old (Looks like 27 )· Moon Elf
Build: Lean, precise — the build of someone who moves silently as a
       default and has never had a reason to stop. Medium height.
       The kind of person you realize has been in the room for
       ten minutes before you noticed.

Face: Moon Elf features — sharp, angular, elongated slightly. High
      cheekbones. Skin with the faint silver-white luminance of
      Moon Elf heritage. Looks early 30s by human standards.
      127 years settled into her as composure, not age. Very beautiful and sexy

Hair: Silver-white, kept back — practical braid or tied. Nothing
      loose that would interfere with archery draw.

Eyes: Silver-grey with the faint luminance that Moon Elf eyes carry.
      The defining feature. The eyes that see things before others
      do — not mystical, just precise. The specific attention
      of someone who reads environments as text.

Expression (default): Still. Not cold — still. Paying attention in
      the specific way that makes people uncomfortable because they
      realize they have been assessed and the assessment is complete.

Armor: Moonweave Leather — lightweight, silent. Forest grey-green
       with matte finish. No metal contact points. No gleam.
       Shadow Cloak over the leather — terrain-adaptive,
       doesn't rustle. She looks like she could become the
       background of wherever she is.

Weapons: Eclipsing Whisper Longbow — legendary moonwood composite, carried strung.
         Silverthorn Daggers — twin short blades at both hips.
         Both are functional, nothing decorative.

Mount/Pet (optional): Nymeris — Eagle . Rare.

Color Palette: Forest grey, silver-white, muted green.
               No bright colors. The palette of someone
               who has spent a century not being seen.

Mood Keywords: Presence without announcement · The arrow already
               nocked · Ancient competence in a young-seeming body ·
               Pays attention more than anyone in the room

Do NOT include: Dramatic elf fantasy styling. She doesn't look
                like a fantasy elf — she looks like a very old,
                very precise person who happens to have elf features.
                No flowing robes, no glowing effects, no dramatic
                lighting choices.

VELKAN DRAKE — Grand Arcanist · Pillar of the Mind · Athenaeum Director
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 49 (Year 1047) · Drakari (draconic bloodline)
Build: Tall, lean — the build of a scholar who is also a battlemage.
       Not physically imposing in the Kael sense — imposing in the
       sense of someone who takes up more conceptual space than
       physical space. The presence of someone who is always
       thinking faster than the conversation.

Face: Drakari features — humanoid but with the specific quality of
      draconic heritage. Slightly scaled texture at the temples and
      jaw (subtle, not monster). Sharpened cheekbones. Eyes that
      are unusual. The face of someone who has been the most
      intelligent person in every room for fifty years and
      finds this moderately interesting.

Skin: Warm-dark, with the faint scale-pattern of Drakari blood
      at the temples, jaw, and back of hands. Not reptilian —
      the texture of old leather in those areas.

Hair: Dark, often neglected — not styled, not a mess, simply
      the hair of someone who has better things to think about.
      Occasionally singed at the ends from proximity to Pyrewing.

Eyes: The key feature. Amber-gold with the vertical slit pupil of
      draconic heritage — when he's idle they look almost round,
      when he's focused they sharpen visibly. Unsettling in the
      most interesting way.

Expression (default): The specific expression of someone who has
      just thought of something three conversational steps ahead.
      Not absent — too present, in the wrong direction. Notebook
      is almost always in hand or nearby.

Clothing/Armor:
  - Field/combat: Runebound Battleplate — silver-blue with rune
                  channels, lighter than standard plate. Carries
                  the look of someone who built their armor around
                  being able to cast while wearing it.
  - Research/default: Dark robes or scholarly coat over the plate,
                      the academic over the soldier.

Weapon: Cinderwake — Dragon Staff. Dark wood with draconic bone
        inlay, runic inscriptions along the shaft, the top
        end showing active but contained fire. Carried constantly.

Mount (optional): Pyrewing — volcanic elite drake. Obsidian-black,
                  40ft wingspan. If shown together: Velkan on
                  Pyrewing looks like the most natural combination
                  of two ancient things.

Color Palette: Deep silver-blue (armor), dark charcoal (robes),
               amber-gold (eyes), dark wood (staff).

Mood Keywords: Intellectual intensity · Draconic heritage made subtle ·
               The scholar who also burns things · Always three steps
               ahead · Notebook first, battle second

Do NOT include: Robes that make him look like a wizard stereotype.
                He has military bearing even in scholarly settings.
                The draconic features should be subtle — texture
                and eyes, not scales covering the face.

SERAPHINE MIRE — Pillar of the Heart · Commander of the Ashen Guard
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: Appears mid-late 30s · Race : Half-Celestial/Half Human
          (keeps this contained — not visually obvious at rest)
Build: Medium, capable — the build of someone who moves through
       active battlefield environments constantly. Not a fighter
       primarily, but the specific fitness of someone who runs
       toward what others run from.

Face: The key quality: warm without performing warmth. The face
      of someone who has been the person who stays when everyone
      else leaves, for years, and still shows up the next morning.
      Clear eyes, steady. The expression that makes panicked
      people slow down.

Hair: Dark, kept practical — back or braid. Nothing that
      would interfere with field work.

Eyes: Dark. The eyes that notice what needs noticing — not
      calculating like Rayden's, receptive. The specific quality
      of someone who actually hears you when you speak.

Expression (default): Present. Fully present. Not warm in a
      performed way — genuinely in the room, with you, assessing
      what you need. The healer's gaze that has been doing this
      long enough to see the answer before the question.

Armor: Ashen Guard Plate — silver-plated reinforced plate with
       purification sigils etched into the joints. The silver
       carries a faint passive light (very subtle — not glowing,
       the specific quality of something consecrated).
       Full-seal helm for field ops, otherwise visible face.

Weapons: Mercy (Sacred Censer) , ceremonial Blade on waist

Color Palette: Silver, white, dark iron (the plate base),
               the very faint warm silver-gold of purification light.
               Clean colors against a field of darker surroundings.

Mood Keywords: Warmth without performance · The person who stays ·
               The healer who also fights · The half-celestial contained ·
               Silver light that doesn't announce itself

Do NOT include: Angelic imagery, wings, dramatic glowing.
                The celestial heritage is interior, not visible.
                She looks like a very capable, very present person
                in functional armor. Not ethereal.

NYX RAVEL — Pillar of Secrets · Commander of the Null Blades
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: Unknown · Shadekin (dark-adapted species, shadow-bonded)
Build: Lean, medium height — the build of someone who is never
       where you expect them to be. No wasted mass. The specific
       fitness of someone for whom speed and silence are survival.

Face: Shadekin features — dark-adapted. The skin has a deep grey-
      charcoal quality, darker than any human baseline. Features
      are sharp but arranged for vanishing — the face that your
      eye slides off of in a crowd. When he wants to be seen,
      he is. When he doesn't, he isn't.

Hair: Dark, close or absent — nothing that creates silhouette.

Eyes: The key feature. Shadekin eyes are adapted for darkness —
      very dark irises with expanded pupils, slight silver reflective
      quality in low light (like a cat's, but subtle). In daylight:
      dark grey, sharp. In shadow: faintly luminous.

Expression (default): Nothing. Not cold, not warm — simply absent.
      The expression of someone who has decided that facial
      expression is information they don't owe anyone. The only
      tell is in the eyes: always assessing, always three steps
      into a plan you haven't started yet.

Armor: Umbral Weave Armor — shadow-dyed layered leather. Absorbs
       light — no reflection, no gleam at any angle. The specific
       black that isn't black, it's the absence of light.
       No identifying marks. No insignia.

Weapons: Shadowfang Daggers — twin short blades, always sheathed
         unless in use. Whisper Crossbow — compact, carried across
         the back. All weapons are dark-finished, no shine.

Mount (optional): Umbra — shadowfang Panther. Pure black with that
                  same light-absorbing quality as Nyx's armor.
                  Silent. If depicted: Umbra at Nyx's side looks
                  like a shadow that chose to have a shape.

Color Palette: Near-black, shadow-grey, the specific dark that
               actively absorbs surrounding light. No bright colors.
               The single visible accent: the silver of his eyes
               in shadow.

Mood Keywords: The absence of presence · Shadow made deliberate ·
               Already knows · The person who was in the room before
               you arrived and will be there after you leave

Do NOT include: Dramatic assassin clichés, visible blades drawn
                constantly, skulking posture. Nyx is more unnerving
                still than dramatic. He looks like nothing until
                he needs to look like something.

THORIN BLACKROOT — Pillar of the Land · Lord of Greenveil
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 41 (Year 1047) · Wildblood Dwarf
Build: The specific build of a Wildblood Dwarf — shorter than human
       standard, but the word for it is not small. Dense, root-heavy,
       the build of something that grew into the ground and doesn't
       move unless it chooses to. Broad shoulders, thick arms,
       the weight distribution of a creature that is part of the
       landscape it stands in.

Face: Dwarven features — broad jaw, strong brow. But the Wildblood
      quality shows in the skin: slightly textured, the colour of
      aged bark in the specific way of someone whose bloodline
      runs through old forest. Not literal bark — the tonal quality
      of it. Beard: substantial, dark with earth-tone streaks,
      braided simply.

Hair: Dark, kept back or braided. Practical — he works in a forest.

Eyes: Deep brown-green. The eyes that read a forest the way Crest
      reads the sea — everything is information, everything is known.

Expression (default): Steady. The stillness of a very old tree.
      Not slow — the stillness that comes after a long time of
      knowing the difference between what requires reaction and
      what doesn't. When he speaks, it's usually one sentence.
      It's usually the correct sentence.

Armor/Clothing: Rootguard Harness — living wood and ironwood armor.
                Adaptive, grown rather than forged. The texture of
                it is organic — not rough, but natural-edge finishing.
                It looks like the forest decided to become armor.
                No metal gleam.

Weapons: Stone-Fist Gauntlets — heavily reinforced earthen-material
         gauntlets that make his already formidable hands into
         weapons. Rootcleaver — Living Axe on the back when in battle mode.

Mount (optional): Grond — alpha dire bear. Enormous, scarred,
                  ancient. When Thorin and Grond are together they
                  look like two things that have come to the same
                  arrangement with the world.

Color Palette: Bark brown, forest green, ironwood dark, earth tones
               throughout. No bright colors — the palette of old
               growth forest.

Mood Keywords: Root-deep stillness · Forest made person · The weight
               of something ancient in a relatively young form ·
               Economy of word and movement · Immovable on principle

Do NOT include: Fantasy dwarf stereotypes — no ale, no forge, no
                underground imagery. Thorin is entirely of the forest.
                He has never looked more at home than in Greenveil.

AURELIA VEX — Pillar of the Sky · Commander of the Stormriders
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 21 (Year 1047) · Storm Elf (Tempestian — storm affinity bloodline)
Build: Athletic, young — the build of someone who has been riding
       dragons since before it was her job. Lean, quick. The specific
       fitness of someone who operates at altitude and needs to hold
       on to a dragon in a storm.

Face: Young — visibly so. The youngest of the Eleven in Year 1047 by
      significant margin. The face of someone who is twenty-one and
      knows it and finds this largely irrelevant to the conversation.
      Bright, sharp, the specific alertness of someone for whom
      everything is either interesting or a problem to solve.
      The kind of face that smiles fast and means it.

Hair: Wind-managed — which is to say, whatever it's doing, the wind
      had input. Dark blonde or light brown. Usually loose or messily
      tied. Perpetually slightly windswept even indoors.

Eyes: The notable feature. Storm-touched — a very specific blue-grey
      that shifts slightly in bright light. Not glowing — just
      the specific color of high-altitude sky.

Expression (default): Active. She doesn't have a resting neutral — she
      has a resting "what's next." The person who is already moving
      before the problem is fully described.

Armor: Storm Rider Plate — specifically designed for electrical
       discharge resistance (Zephyros is a living storm). Articulated
       for full aerial range of motion. Lighter than standard plate.
       Weather-worn — she lives outside.
       High-altitude sealing at the joints.

Weapons: Storm Spear — carried or strapped. Electro-Blades — twin
         short blades at the hips. Both carry the visual texture
         of weapons that have been used, repeatedly, in actual storms.

Mount: Zephyros — Asiatic Elder Storm Dragon. Several hundred years
       old. Massive. Blue-grey-white coloring. Storm-threaded air
       around him. When Aurelia and Zephyros are together:
       she looks tiny against him, which is accurate, and somehow
       also entirely in command, which is also accurate.

Color Palette: Storm blue-grey, white, silver. The palette of
               high-altitude weather. Her armor has a weathered
               quality — not rusted, used.

Mood Keywords: Young and entirely aware of it · The sky is her
               default environment · Fast decisions · The elder
               dragon and the young woman who are somehow equal ·
               Combat makes her focused not afraid

Do NOT include: Delicate features, court dress, any suggestion that
                she is ornamental. She looks like she just landed
                from a dragon and is already planning the next flight.

IRAE DUSKWIND — "The Galloping Storm" · Commander
                of the Windlance Cavalry
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 23 · Half Moon Elf

Build: Lean, athletic, built for the specific discipline she
       invented — the body of someone who spent years training
       in two completely different schools (Warden marksmanship
       and cavalry horsemanship) at once and fused them into one
       new thing. Looks like she's already moving even at rest.

Face: Half Moon Elf features — sharp, angular, the faint
      silver-white luminance of Moon Elf heritage filtered
      through human proportions, giving her a slightly warmer,
      more weathered look than a full Moon Elf. The face of
      someone who arrived at Starhold's training grounds already
      certain the standards would be insufficient — and was right.

Hair: Practical, plains-rider style — kept back or braided,
      built to survive a full gallop without becoming a problem.
      Dark with a faint silvered quality at certain angles
      (the Moon Elf trait, understated).Bob Cut hairstyle with dark highlights.

Eyes: Sharp, green-toned or pale, with the faint Moon Elf
      luminance. The eyes of someone who can track a moving
      target from a galloping horse with Warden-level precision —
      always reading distance, angle, and wind at once.

Expression (default): Focused intensity edged with quiet
      satisfaction — the look of someone who built something that
      didn't exist before and knows exactly what it's worth.
      Not arrogant — certain. "Warden precision. Cavalry speed.
      Nobody had put them together before — so I did."

Armor/Clothing: Windlance Cavalry command gear — lightweight,
       saddle-optimized armor that prioritizes mobility and full
       range of motion for mounted archery over raw protection.
       Green-toned accents (#489020) marking her command rank.
       Built for someone who lives on horseback.

Weapons: Windbow — short recurve, saddle-optimized, her signature
         weapon (alongside the recurve horsebow she arrived with,
         "half her height"). Saber carried for emergency melee.
         Silent Warden training blades from her Warden years and Small cavalry shield on back/side.

Mount: Swiftmere — grey plains-bred warhorse of exceptional
       speed. The horse and rider read as a single unit in
       motion; she does not so much ride as extend through it.

Color Palette: Plains green (#489020 accent), grey (matching
               Swiftmere's coat), warm earth and leather tones,
               the faint cool silver-white of Moon Elf heritage
               at the edges.

Mood Keywords: Warden precision, cavalry speed — fused into
               something new · "We do not chase. We are already
               where you are going." · Walked two hundred miles
               to be assessed by Lyria, and Lyria put down her
               own bow · The cavalry equivalent of a bow ·
               Six hundred riders, one heartbeat

Do NOT include: Delicate or ornamental styling, heavy ceremonial
                armor that would slow mounted archery, anything
                that separates her visually from her horse and
                bow — those two things are inseparable from her
                identity. She is defined by motion and precision
                combined, not by presence at rest.

DR. ISOLDE CREN — "The Hands That Hold" · Chief Physician ·
                  Hospital Network Director · Apothecary Corps
                  Founder
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 45 (Year 1047) · Human · Female

Build: The build of someone who has been doing physical medicine —
       not magic, not casting, hands-in medicine — for twenty
       years in frontier conditions. Capable, precise, neither
       the administrative softness of a court physician nor the
       battlefield bulk of a combat medic. The specific body of
       a person who treats trauma without magical backup because
       there is none available and the patient is still alive.

Face: Direct, attentive, the face of someone who monitors
      Seraphine Mire's accumulated physical cost from Martyr's
      Embrace use and has told no one — not because she is cold,
      but because that is her professional standard. Clear-eyed.
      The expression of a physician who asks "why" as a reflex,
      not a question.

Hair: Practical — kept back from the face entirely, the clinical
      standard of someone who works over patients. Warmer tone,
      auburn or dark brown, going silver at the edges.

Eyes: Clinical attentiveness — reading patients the way Lyria
      reads terrain. The eyes that looked at a decade's worth of
      misattributed Void-touch complications and immediately asked
      why nobody had collected that data before.

Expression (default): Composed professional focus. Not cold —
      the specific warmth of someone who chose medicine because
      she was good at it and has been right about that choice for
      twenty years. When she disagrees with a clinical decision,
      she says so directly. When she is right, she does not
      remind anyone.

Clothing: Chief Physician's working kit — practical layered
       garments over a physician's professional coat, clinical
       instrument belt (the apothecary tools, the diagnostic
       equipment, always present). Not the Ashen Guard's silver
       purification aesthetic — Isolde's palette is the warm
       rose-crimson (#993556) of the Sancthaven medical domain,
       applied in practical professional clothing rather than
       armor. A physician's coat that has been in frontier
       conditions and been cleaned without becoming ceremonial.

Color Palette: Warm rose-crimson (#993556 accent — Sancthaven
               medical), dark neutral base tones, the clean
               clinical palette of someone who treats physical
               wounds in a domain where everything else is magical.

Mood Keywords: "Because no one was looking." · Monitors
               Seraphine weekly and has told no one · Arrived
               with research, not an application · Free basic
               medical care across all settlements — her design ·
               Stopped two Iron Star fights on safety grounds —
               both fighters wrote thank-you letters · Her
               clinical disagreements with her husband Jeren are
               the most productive arguments in Sancthaven

Do NOT include: Cold or emotionally distant doctor stereotype.
                She is warm — rigorously warm, the warmth of
                someone who cares precisely and completely.
                No elaborate robes or ceremonial dress; she
                looks like she is about to treat someone, because
                she usually is.

VESPERA DRAKEWYNN — "The Storm Before the Charge" · Captain of
                    the Black Charge · Elite Cavalry Commander
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 25 (Year 1047) · Nightborn Human · Female

Build: The build of someone who defeated every veteran candidate
       in the Black Charge trials and received command at
       twenty-five. Not the heaviest cavalry build — the most
       precisely devastating. The physicality of a cavalry general
       whose parents were both legendary commanders on a different
       continent, and who trained in the doctrine they left her.

Face: Striking — not ornamental, battlefield-striking. The
      specific quality of a face that projects charisma at range,
      the kind that works equally on allies and enemies. Fierce,
      sharp, certain of itself in the way of someone who has
      never once doubted whether she belonged in a command role.
      Nightborn human features — slightly deeper skin tone and
      the specific quality of a lineage adapted to dark-climate
      environments, warm-dark rather than pale. True beauty with Brain.

Hair: Dark, windswept — what it becomes after a charge. Managed
      enough for the helmet, loose enough to read as someone
      who is always just back from or about to go on one.

Eyes: Dark, fierce, focused. The eyes of someone for whom combat
      reads as a targeting problem with an obvious solution —
      not reckless, fast. She decides before others have finished
      describing the situation.

Expression (default): Fierce confidence that is not arrogance —
      the specific assurance of someone who earned what she
      has by demonstrating it against every veteran who said
      she couldn't. The look that makes armies break formation
      before the lance lands.

Armor: Black cavalry plate — full combat armor designed for shock
       charge rather than siege defense, articulated for maximum
       mounted mobility. Dark iron, battle-worn from trials and
       field command. The Black Charge's dark aesthetic — no
       bright insignia, just the weight of what's coming.

Weapons: Ruinspike — shadowsteel cavalry lance, long, forged
         to pierce heavy armor and break lines at full charge.
         Grimtalon — curved saber at the hip for mounted close
         combat after the charge. Ashfang Pistols — twin rune-
         flint cavalry hand cannons at both hips, inherited from
         her mother; dark-finished, worn-grip, the look of
         weapons that have been used and kept. She fires them
         once per charge, at the moment of maximum impact.
         Never missed.

Mount: RedMane — crimson armored warhorse bred for shock charges
       and sustained battlefield endurance, selected personally
       by Marcus Ironmane for her command style. The specific
       red-armored coloring makes it visible from distance — this
       is tactical, not decorative. When depicted with Vespera:
       she looks like the storm, and RedMane is the ground
       it moves on.

Color Palette: Black armor, dark iron, the deep crimson (#6A1010)
               of her cavalry's mark and RedMane's coat.
               Shadowsteel lance-sheen (dark, not silver).
               The palette of something arriving fast from
               a direction you were not watching.

Mood Keywords: "You hear us before you see us. By the time you
               see us, the decision is already made." · Earned
               command at 25 — no one mentions this twice ·
               Her parents' continent is gone. Starhaven is the
               first thing since that is not grief. She rides
               like she means to keep it. · Fires the Ashfang
               Pistols once per charge, at maximum impact, has
               never missed

Do NOT include: Ornamental or decorative cavalry aesthetics,
                anything that reads as "princess on horseback."
                She looks like what she is — a cavalry commander
                who defeated every veteran in the kingdom to
                earn her position, whose parents were war legends,
                and who fights like she was trained in a different
                war than everyone around her. The darkness and
                the crimson are the visual truth, not a costume.

WYLD FROSTMERE — "The Cold Wall" · Frosthold Bastion Commander ·
                 Northern Border Defense · Blizzard Combat
                 Specialist
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 41 (Year 1047) · Dwarf · Male

Build: Dwarf — the dense, root-heavy build of the Dwarven line,
       but expressed as the specific physicality of someone who
       lives in Frosthold's northern posting year-round, by
       choice, because nobody else wanted it. Built for cold
       more than combat — the endurance-weight of someone whose
       garrison discipline is the weather itself.

Face: Weathered, dry-humored — the face of someone who sent
      three incursion attempts back as weather disasters, wrote
      the after-action reports anyway, and considered it
      "unsporting to call them battles." The lines of someone
      who has been standing in blizzards at Frosthold for years
      and finds them professionally useful.

Hair/Beard: Dwarven-traditional, dark with frost-grey threading
      from the posting's climate. Beard practical rather than
      elaborate — the beard of a garrison commander who goes
      outside in actual blizzards.

Eyes: Calm, assessing — the eyes of someone who reads wind
      direction and incoming weather the way other commanders
      read enemy formations, because at Frosthold they are
      the same information. The annotation he added to his
      military academy course materials ("The part about wind
      direction is wrong") lives in these eyes.

Expression (default): Dry, patient satisfaction. The look of
      someone waiting for the moment the enemy makes the mistake
      he has already accounted for — which at Frosthold is
      always the moment they underestimate the cold.

Armor: Northern garrison plate — Dwarven cold-weather reinforced,
       maximum protection variant with layered insulation
       underneath. Built for sustained exposure to Frosthold's
       conditions rather than rapid deployment. Anti-corruption
       treatment on the helm (northern Void-touch environments
       require it). Matte blue-grey finish, frost-worn.

Weapons: Permafrost — frost-tempered war-axe, his signature
         weapon. Frost-treated blade with the specific look of
         metal that has been exposed to extreme cold for years
         and maintained anyway.

Mount: Snowtusk — armored mountain elk. The northern posting's
       standard for commanders who actually go outside in
       blizzards. Elk-large, sure-footed on ice, the practical
       choice rather than the dramatic one.

Color Palette: Deep cold blue (#185FA5 accent), matte garrison
               grey, frost-white, the northern bastion palette.
               The colors of a fortress built into a place where
               the weather is the primary military asset.

Mood Keywords: "The blizzard is my garrison. I just give it
               direction." · Requested the posting no one wanted ·
               Kael Varric reviewed two years of reports and
               replied: "Correct." — Wyld had it framed. It is
               the finest professional recognition of his career.
               · Three incursion attempts dissolved as weather
               disasters. Zero conventional engagement required.
               He considers this a gift. He receives it gratefully.

Do NOT include: Heroic battle-ready posture or dramatic lighting.
                Wyld Frostmere is functional, patient, and dry —
                the garrison commander whose most powerful weapon
                is a storm he didn't make and learned to use. He
                looks like the cold wall the title says he is,
                not a heroic figure against it.

SORIN — Wing Commander · Sub-Commander of the Ninth Sky Corps (Operations)
────────────────────────────────────────
Age/Race: 39 · Drakari (draconic bloodline),male

Build: Lean, hard-muscled — a drake rider's build, built for
       wind-resistance and grip strength rather than raw bulk.
       Sits straighter than most, even on the ground, the
       posture of someone permanently braced against a dive.

Face: Drakari features — humanoid with a faint scaled texture at
      the temples, jaw, and the backs of the hands (subtle, not
      monster). Sharper, harder-edged than Velkan's scholarly
      version of the same bloodline — Sorin's draconic traits
      read as a flight-predator's, not a thinker's.
      Petite Goate beard, red.

Hair: Bald, have a tatoo on right side abve ear.

Eyes: Amber-gold with the vertical slit pupil of draconic
      heritage, like Velkan's but harder — narrowed habitually,
      the eyes of someone scanning formation gaps. They track
      movement before they settle on a face.

Expression (default): Flat, assessing — the look of a man who
      vets every rider in the Corps personally and has never
      stopped doing the math on who lives and who doesn't.
      Not cold exactly — controlled. Burns instead of bends.

Armor/Clothing: Ninth Sky Corps drake-rider harness-plate —
      flight-rigged armor with reinforced leg straps and a
      high collar against wind, blue-grey metal with rune
      channels running down the forearms and chest that glow
      faint orange when his fire-arcane is active. Lighter
      and more wind-cut than Velkan's Runebound Battleplate,
      built for being strapped to a drake's back at speed.

Weapons/Tools: Emberstave Staff and a rune-channel lance — the
      lance is the signature piece, a long drake-rider's lance
      with glowing rune-channels along its length that flare
      bright orange-red when he releases a Flareburst. The
      staff is carried as backup/ground weapon, more compact
      than Velkan's Cinderwake.


Mount (optional): A Ninth Sky Corps drake — if shown, a
      battle-drake(wvyren) in corps colors (blue-grey harness over
      dark scales), built for fast formation flying rather
      than Pyrewing's volcanic bulk.

Color Palette: Blue-grey (#4070B0) Ninth Sky Corps colors as the
      primary accent, with arcane fire-orange glow along the
      rune channels of his lance and armor. Dark scaled skin
      tones at temples/jaw/hands. Overall cooler and more
      metallic than Velkan's warm dark-academic palette.

Mood Keywords: Burns instead of bends · Operations commander,
               not a frontline showpiece · Personally vetted
               every rider since the War of Starhold · The kind
               of competence that doesn't need to announce itself

Do NOT include: Scholarly or academic styling — Sorin is a
                field operations commander, not a researcher.
                No robes, no notebook. He should read as a
                hardened drake-rider in flight gear, harder-edged
                and more military than Velkan's version of the
                same Drakari bloodline.
