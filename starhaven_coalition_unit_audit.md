## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

**Active:** Soldiers currently deployed in the field
**Reserve:** Held back — rotation, replacement, reinforcement
**Subunit of:** Parent command or Pillar this unit reports to
**NEW:** Unit created during or after the Vael'Dorum campaign

### Reserve Categories
- **A — 30% reserve:** Large conventional units
- **B — 25% reserve:** Specialized units
- **C — 20% reserve:** Elite units
- **D — 15% reserve:** Specialist doctrine units
- **E — No reserve:** Irreplaceable assets
