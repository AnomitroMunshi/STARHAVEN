# STARHAVEN — Story TL;DR (for Claude and other readers)

Read this instead of the 9 chapter files (`Lores/chapters/Starhaven_Chapter1–9.html`, ~320,000 words) when you need to know **what happens in the main story**. For details of a person, place or unit, the wikis are the source of truth (`starhaven_wiki.html`; units: `starhaven-unit-wiki.html`; enemies: `nullborn_enemy_units.html`).

## Ground rules

- **Timeline.** Chapters run **Year 1031 → 1049** (Sundered Age). The wiki and most chronicles are set later, around **Year 1059**; a few standalone legend records are set during the chapter years. Things only in the wiki are not automatically "lore gaps."
- **Never rename a name that appears in a chapter** (exception made once by the user: Clea Vorn → Clea Halvorsen, Chapter 7).
- **Names.** Vorn Ashcaller is the only person called Vorn; Aldric and Mira Vorn (Ironmere's king and queen) keep the surname. New names come from cultures worldwide, and no two people share a first name.
- **Pronouns.** Check the card. Nyx Ravel is he/him. Renzo Fal is she/her. Wyld Frostmere is he/him. Senna Ash is female.
- † = dies. (?) = fate unknown.

## Chapter 1 — The Boy Who Ran Through Fire · Years 1031–1036
**Places:** Oaksmane (orphanage, factory slums) → Westreach (Adventurers' Guild) → Hollowwatch ruins.
**First appear:** Rayden Cale, Garrick Cale †, Marianne Vale †, Caelum ("Kai" — presumed dead for years), Lord Halbrecht Veyne, Elira Voss, Selene Voss, the Hollowwatch party (Dorun Graymaw †, Korr Vash †, Nyssa Veil (?), Tharel Ethen), the Temporal Rift party (Lysara Quill, Varik Stonevein).
1. 1031: Rayden (11) lives in Saint Veyne orphanage, Oaksmane; Marianne Vale runs it, the soldier Garrick Cale protects it in secret and trains Rayden.
2. Halbrecht Veyne ("the Lantern of Oaksmane"), a respected noble, orders the **Night of Fire**: the orphanage burns, 23 children and Marianne die. Garrick carries Rayden out.
3. Garrick is publicly executed for opposing Halbrecht. Rayden learns to hate.
4. 1032: starving and sick, Rayden is found by **Elira Voss**, a hard old adventurer, who trains him with her daughter **Selene**. Gift: the **Vossfang Bracer** (chain weapon).
5. Westreach Guild: Rayden and Selene become **the Wall-Climber** (he climbs monsters) and **the Lantern Veil** (a healer who fights on the front line).
6. 1036, **Hollowwatch**: Rayden wins **Dawnpiercer** (relic sword and shield). Dorun Graymaw dies holding the collapse; Korr Vash dies.
7. He awakens **Temporal Frenzy** (moves while time slows); it almost kills him (convulsions, bleeding eyes). War is coming.

## Chapter 2 — Ashes Before Kingdoms · Years 1037–1039
**Places:** Lowmere Field, Blackwall Ridge, Red Orchard Valley, Fort Asterfall, Veyr Kharos (dead kingdom), Veynehold.
**First appear:** Velkan Drake (the Ashen Scholar) and Pyrewing, Darian Wolfe.
1. Dungeon work dries up; Rayden and Selene become mercenaries in the frontier wars.
2. **Battle of Lowmere Field** (1037): rain and slaughter; Rayden's war-names spread. Darian Wolfe shares their camp, then fights them from the other side.
3. **Siege of Blackwall Ridge**: Rayden glimpses Velkan Drake on Pyrewing.
4. **Red Orchard Engagement** (1038): Rayden turns a burning convoy into a trap and destroys a cavalry force. Corruption and plague spread south.
5. **Fort Asterfall** (1039): **Selene Voss † dies holding a falling wall so the refugees beneath it live.** Rayden is a few steps too late; he trains Temporal Frenzy obsessively afterwards. (Selene Orchard in Starhold is later built for her: "somewhere nobody needed swords".)
6. In the ruins of **Veyr Kharos** Rayden takes **Kingsbane**, a black-and-gold execution greatsword.
7. Elira slaps him for what he is becoming.
8. **Veynehold**: Rayden destroys Halbrecht's prison and slave town and frees the captives — "the first foundation of Starhaven". (Wiki: he reached Halbrecht with Kingsbane and lowered it — "live long enough to see a better world replace yours". Halbrecht is alive.)
9. Epilogue: Velkan studies the corruption — it is not random; **it is searching**.

## Chapter 3 — The Wandering Legends · Years 1039–1041
**Places:** the eastern roads, Frostmere trade road, a mountain monastery, the Hollow Frontier, the Ironmane farm, **Havenfire**.
**First appear:** Kael Varric, Lyria Valen, Daven Holt, Vera (Marsh) Holt, Marcus · Helna · Dresh Ironmane, Corin.
1. Rayden walks east alone after the corruption, protecting refugees.
2. He meets **Kael Varric** (Stoneborn, tower shield GraveTide), who is holding a ruined checkpoint so civilians can escape. They become partners.
3. Black Rain ambush; at a monastery Rayden finally reads **Selene's journal** and grieves.
4. **Lyria Valen** (127-year-old Moon Elf huntress, bow Eclipsing Whisper) joins.
5. The Hollow Frontier fills with refugees of every race; carved warnings from the Ashen Scholar say the corruption could fracture the continent within twenty years.
6. They save the **Ironmane farm** (Marcus, Helna, Dresh, warhorses, five chickens), who join.
7. They take an old fortress and name it **Havenfire** (1041), the first safe place. Rayden decides to find the Ashen Scholar.

## Chapter 4 — The Gathering Storm · Year 1041
**Places:** Havenfire, **Vaelmark** (the arcane city), the frontier.
**First appear:** Seraphine Mire, Sorin Vael (Ninth Sky Corps), Brek Stonefall, Dresh Ironmane (as a fighter).
1. Rayden goes to Vaelmark and meets **Velkan Drake**; Velkan's maps show the corruption is **studying how civilizations survive**.
2. The **Silverfire Trial** in Vaelmark; news arrives: Havenfire is under full assault (corrupted horde, **Siegeborn Titans**).
3. Kael holds the wall and is struck through with corruption — dying.
4. Rayden and Lyria ride to find help; **Seraphine Mire, the Saint of Ash**, reaches Havenfire and purifies Kael.
5. Velkan and Seraphine stay. **The Cleansing March**: the protectors' first counter-attack together.

## Chapter 5 — The Birth of Starhold · Year 1042
**Places:** Havenfire (10,000+ people), Rookthorn, the Sable Isles (by letter), Greenveil and the Greywood, Moonfall Lake, the hilltop fortress (later Starhold).
**First appear:** Nyx Ravel, Thorin Blackroot and Grond, Renzo Fal, Dren Solm, Lena Crest, Kai, Sorvane Nullis, **Warlord Drevak**, Gordin.
1. Havenfire is overcrowded; Kael: "the walls are wrong". They need a real city — the **hilltop fortress held by Warlord Drevak** beside the fallen star.
2. **Rookthorn**: an unseen killer wipes out cult leaders and tests what Rayden protects — **Nyx Ravel**, who joins on his own terms.
3. **Sable Conclave crisis**: Velkan and Seraphine answer the necromancers' call; **Sorvane Nullis** allies with Vaelmark and Havenfire.
4. **Dren Solm** arrives — the chronicler who writes everything down.
5. **Greenveil catastrophe**: Sorin is wounded; the Greywood is dying. At **Moonfall Lake** they find **Thorin Blackroot** grieving and fight beside him. Greenveil and Vaelmark join.
6. Three armies march on the fortress. Epilogues: Drevak watches them come and laughs; Nyx kills his scouts.

## Chapter 6 — The War of Starhold · Year 1042
**Places:** Greenveil muster, the Greywood road, the hilltop fortress, the corrupted meteor beneath it.
**First appear:** Garruk Blackroot † (Thorin's brother), Senna Ash, Rhegar Thorne (last page), Cyren.
1. The coalition musters at Greenveil; Nyx's intelligence: ~50,000 defenders, anti-drake ballistae, kill corridors, cult tunnels.
2. The assault on the outer walls; at nightfall **eight corrupted Titans** come out.
3. **Garruk Blackroot dies**; Thorin fights on in beast form. Sorvane's undead army fights beside the living.
4. Seraphine goes down to the **corrupted meteor (the falling star)** under the hill.
5. **Rayden kills Drevak** in close combat (Ruinfall-charged blows through his armour, then a neck break). (Wiki: the Nullborn registry later *reports* Drevak returned — **unconfirmed** in Starhaven; card status "unknown".)
6. The fortress becomes **Starhold**. The people choose Rayden as king; the kingdom is named **Starhaven, Year 1042**.
7. Final page: Rhegar Thorne, far across the sea, opens a letter from Starhold.

## Chapter 7 — A New Beginning · Years 1043–1046 (longest chapter)
**Places:** Starhold, Ironpass, Greenveil, Vaelmark, Learnstead, Ashfield, Dawncliff, Sancthaven, the **Vrak Isles**.
**First appear (main):** Aurelia Vex and Zephyros, Elysia Nocturne, Cain Drakhar, Veyra Tal, Doran Ashe, Brek Tane, Brynhild Voss, Astrya Lenn, Althea Sorn, Cyrus Vexley, Aldren Hander, Drev Holt, Tiber Oswick, Valtor Crane, Crest and Seren Vane, King Aldric and Queen Mira Vorn (Ironmere), Wyld Frostmere, Isolde Cren, Clea Halvorsen, Irae Duskwind, Ela Seawall, Kira (giant), and many commanders.
1. Starhold grows to ~30,000 under the **Open Visa** policy. **Rhegar Thorne** arrives with Ironmere veterans; the **First Field Army** forms; Starhaven begins producing its own commanders (Clea Halvorsen).
2. **Ashfield**: a wrong thing shows the Void has been watching Starhaven since before it had a name.
3. **Learnstead** and schools under Althea Sorn; **Renzo Fal's** fourth rift-gate attempt finally works. **Selene Orchard** (no blades) is built.
4. **The Year the Sky Changed** (1045): Aurelia Vex and the storm dragon Zephyros arrive over Starhold. Cyrus Vexley's crystal railway is demonstrated.
5. **The Seven Days of Starhaven**: ministers formally appointed, units introduced ("The Named"), the old companions drink in the Old House tavern.
6. **What the Sea Carries / The Third Chain** (1046): the Thalassian League and an expedition to the **Vrak Isles** (Rayden, Seraphine, Nyx, Daven, Aurelia, Crest Vane, Valtor Crane). The **flying fortress** (pre-Sundering Vrak construct that hunts the Void) fires on Void ground; Zephyros knocks out its main weapons, Aurelia cuts its secondary conduit; it does not retaliate, follows the fleet four hours ("It's remembering us"), and returns east. Chapter 7 mentions more than one fortress. They return with a weapon that waited three years for its owner and **a name for the enemy**. (Rule: no chronicles set in the Vrak Isles; the fortress may appear elsewhere.)

## Chapter 8 — The Eleven Stars · Years 1047–1048
**Places:** Forgehaven, Vaelmark, the Golden Coast Federation, **Saltmere**, Karath, Greenveil, **Lumenfield**, Starhold.
**First appear:** Bob Vale, Zareth Kull and his team (Cray, Illen, Wex, Sorn), Sultan Rashid and Sultana Aria al-Kavar, Tarven al-Soran, Petra Allus, Calvan Eres, Orreth Vayne, Solen Aure, Soltherion Vael, Cren Voss, Sira, Roen.
1. **Iron and Archive**: Cain Drakhar and Cyrus Vexley in Forgehaven take on a new project ("the vessel that goes down").
2. **The Golden Coast Federation** sends its fleet against Starhaven; it lands at **Saltmere**; after eleven days of fighting (Doran Ashe) the Federation becomes a **debt-ally**.
3. **The Bond That Was Already There**: Bob Vale's **Soul Link** joins all eleven Pillars and makes Starhaven's armies far more effective; the Resonance faculty is founded.
4. **Karath and the Gift**: Sultana Aria writes to Rayden personally; Karath's gift (war mammoths) reaches Greenveil.
5. **The Lumenfield Quiet**: Zareth Kull's Void Breakers and Petra Allus on the Void frontier.
6. **The SS+ dungeon** at Lumenfield (first attempt: 41 adventurers in, 31 out) is cleared.
7. **The Trial of the Eleven** (1048): the Pillars fight each other to know what they are.
8. **The Naming**: before the known world, the Eleven are named the Pillars of Starhaven; Dren Solm plays what he cannot write.

## Chapter 9 — What the Mountains Sent · Year 1049
**Places:** Saltmere, Vaelmark, Ironmane Fields, Greenveil and the Greywood, the Greypeak foothills, the Stormmassif.
**First appear:** Vespera Drakewynn, **Gorvak Doomhide †**, Drogar Stonefang, Kethra Blackfang, Drokha Bloodtusk, Kragh Skullsplitter, Kaszrek, Korga, Threska, Oren, Bren Oakshield, Dena Veil, Brokk Graniteborn, Durak Stonemark, Skreel †, Vrennach †, Karsh (Redspire war-chief).
1. **Peacetime works**: six railway lines, twenty-two rift gates, the Dawnfleet, Ironpass finished (1048). But animals, goblins and orcs are fleeing the **Stormmassif**.
2. Thorin feels the **Greypeak foothills dying**: the Void is eating the highlands; ~40,000 highland people have lost their homes.
3. **Gorvak Doomhide** (400-year-old troll warlord) leads the **Highland Horde** on Greenveil.
4. Day 1: the Greenveil Defense Force holds the plain. The **Greywood itself** traps the horde in shifting paths ("The Empty Flank").
5. **Drogar Stonefang** turns his rebuilt Stonefang Clan against Gorvak and **kills him in single combat on Day 4**; Kethra Blackfang and eleven clans kneel to Drogar.
6. Day 7: Rayden rides out himself; the **Highland Coalition** becomes Starhaven's ally — "something that might one day become a kind of family."

**Parallel 1049 chronicle — "Worldsplitter":** Bjorr Runebane meets Rayden on a snowbound Coldmarch pass, where Rayden stands between forty refugee families and a pack of frost-wolves. Bjorr helps drive the wolves off, but does not join Rayden. They bid farewell; the chronicle does not record where Bjorr goes. Filed as a Solo Legend Record in the wiki Chronicles; this meeting is not a scene in Chapter 9.

Eleven days: Doran Ashe holds the third choke; the Soul Link's first war (Hollow Choir caught by Bob in 6 minutes); the Pale General targets the Link; Nyx's Null Blades kill 14 Coordinators; Cain loses an Iron Tide tank to an arcing bolt and redesigns the armour; Brix Holt's slow towers lead to the six-second bridge; Kai drops three Titans at eleven seconds each; War Bears and Null Bears pause before fighting; the Severim and the Unmeasured first seen; Clea's cavalry closes the plain, 11 of 15 Ashwalker companies sit down; Rayden throws Grath'Uel into the Passage; Sera folds away. Mirova shows Brondrith Hurnevik's mithril letters; he opens Highcairn's Senka to Durak. Yaroslava dies closing her own gate on Hurnevik.
