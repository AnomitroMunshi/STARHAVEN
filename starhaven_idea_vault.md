## 1. Arc: The Silver Isle War (working title) — set after Year 1059

**Premise [Ano]:** A land/island with a huge silver mine. Starhaven's spymaster finds it. Another (new) kingdom wants it too, got there first, and is killing the natives or enslaving them. Starhaven fights for the silver, makes a deal with the natives, frees them from slavery, and the island comes under Starhaven's protection.

**Beats [Ano]:**
1. Refugees from the island reach Starhaven.
2. Through them, the spymaster learns about the mine and what is happening there.
3. Starhaven attacks the occupier.
4. Starhaven makes a deal with the natives: silver in return for freedom from slavery.
5. The island becomes part of Starhaven's protection.
6. During the war, **Dante** fights as a mercenary on the natives' side. It is his birthplace, and the natives were already fighting. Starhaven fights alongside him, Rayden likes him, and asks him to join Starhaven.

**The four-armed people [Ano]:** A new race lives on the island. Their original island sank. Their king is unknown and has not been found. He is "the Architect" and is found later.

**Link to the Severim [Ano — "same one"]:** The Architect is connected to the Void's four-armed Void Architects (the Severim in the Nullborn registry). Their origin is still to be decided. See open questions.

**Why it fits the wiki (checked) [Suggested]:**
- Silver: Starhaven buys almost all of its silver from House Silverhorn's Greymantle mine. A second source removes a single-supplier risk. Gold depends largely on the Sable Gold Concession, held under Sorvane Nullis's mining rights.
- Slavery and freedom match the Worker Rights Compact (Year 1043).
- Veyra Tal's *Treaty Before the War* ability and the Fourth Field Army suit the deal-making half. Dawnfleet and Tidebreaker Corps suit the landing.

**Cast:**
- **Tolliver Quarrow** — the spymaster who finds the mine (see section 3).
- **Dante** — merc, native son, later joins Starhaven (section 2).
- **Rayden Cale** — meets Dante in the field.
- **The Architect** — the unknown four-armed king, found later.
- **[Suggested]** Veyra Tal / Fourth Field Army for the treaty; Dawnfleet for the sea landing.

**Open questions:**
- Island name (no "Ash"; check the wiki).
- New rival kingdom's name, ruler and slave system.
- How the Architect connects to the Severim (the island people corrupted by the Void? A separate origin that Starhaven mistakes for them?). The Severim "appeared at Ashfield with no documented ancestor" in the registry, so one of those two wordings would need to change.
- Does the sunken island connect to the Vrak? (Possible, not decided.)
- Does Tolliver come from Treasury (Astrya Lenn) or from Nyx's Web?
- Is House Silverhorn told? It is an ally and the current silver supplier.

## 3. Queued characters (pitched, not built)

All three were name-checked clean on 2026-11-23.

**Selka Rennet — the dead-line signal officer**
- Resonance Corps field officer, under Orin Dael's Link relay.
- Gap: the Soul Link is attacked by the Hollow Choir, Resonance Eaters and the Malgrath-Touched Titan. No one runs a whole army without it.
- Builds a fallback system of banners, lantern codes, drums and runners, plus "dead-line drills" (pre-agreed plans). Detects Link failure from lag.
- Hook: the Hollow Choir's delayed-correct commands would be caught, because her system doesn't use the Link.

**Tolliver Quarrow — the prospector-spymaster**
- Treasury agent under Astrya Lenn, working with Nyx's Web.
- Former assayer who reads ore, ground and rumor. Cover: harmless mapmaker.
- Finds the Silver Isle (section 1).

**Dunmar Underlow — the deep-warden**
- Chief of cistern, well and tunnel defense. Works with Olla Marsh and the Stonemark counter-tunnelers.
- Gap: the wiki has tunnel offense (Veld Ashe, Crex) and tunnel detection (Tunnel-Voice Rusk), but no one defending a living city against Void Burrowers and Hollow Crawlers.
- Seals water routes, salts drains with sacred ash, runs night sweeps, knows every bricked-up shaft. Echoes the Duren's Keep cistern attack.

## 6. More ideas from wiki gaps [Suggested — added 2026-11-23]

Found by counting how thinly the wiki covers each topic. Top picks: **#2 and #7**.


1. **Traitor in the Web (top pick).** The wiki has no "treason" or "rebellion." Every threat is external. Arc: a loyal-looking minister quietly sells information to King's End or Vel'Nathar, and Nyx must hunt a traitor inside his own network.
2. **Malgrath himself.** The Void's king has only about 6 mentions in the main wiki. Possible: Drevak's second return, or someone who has spoken with Malgrath. Pays off the Pale General's sealed document.
3. **Cost of war.** The wiki has the Three-Minute Guarantee, orphanages and the Worker Rights Compact, but no veteran or war-widow story. Character: a veteran advocate pushing for pensions, or a Void-survivor leader. Adds economic strain.
4. **The next generation.** Year 1059's "Unlicensed Party" (Tarin Varric, Leo Holt, Liana Holt, Roen, Tavi Dusk, plus a dire wolf pup) growing up in the 1060s could be a sequel arc.
5. **Moral dilemma for Rayden.** The rival kingdom from the Silver Isle arc offers peace if Starhaven gives up the island. Tests whether "protection" is just conquest.
6. **The Vrak and the flying fortress (top pick).** About 28 mentions of the Vrak. The fortress "remembered" the fleet, and no one knows whether more exist or whether the Nullborn know. Arc: Starhaven tries to contact them. Possible tie to the Silver Isle's sunken island (see section 1).
7. **Silver cold war with House Silverhorn.** Once Starhaven has its own silver, its ally loses leverage. A political arc with no battles, balancing the war-heavy chronicle.
